[Google Shape;42;p6]: 03
[Google Shape;43;p6]: Context / Background
[Google Shape;45;p6]: Electronic Training Record (ETR)  •  SU26SE104_GSU48
[Google Shape;46;p6]: Industry Regulations: Civil aviation demands absolute safety, traceability, and uncompromising compliance with international and national standards (CAAV Part 10, ICAO Annex 1, FAA, EASA).

Multi-Stage Training Lifecycle: Aviation trainees (Pilots, Cabin Crew, Maintenance Technicians) undergo a continuous 3-phase journey:
[Google Shape;47;p6]: Ground School:
Theoretical foundations.
[Google Shape;48;p6]: Flight Operations:
Actual aircraft training and line checks.
[Google Shape;49;p6]: Mandatory Record Retention: Authorities mandate that all attendance, flight hours, examination sheets, instructor endorsements, and certifications must be securely preserved for 5 to 10 years for unannounced safety audits.
[Google Shape;50;p6]: Simulator (SIM / FTD):
Cockpit procedures and emergency handling.
[Google Shape;51;p6]: Problem Statement: Traditional aviation training record management relies heavily on paper logbooks and fragmented spreadsheets, creating massive data tampering risks and audit vulnerabilities.

[Google Shape;58;p7]: Each issue traced to the impact it creates for the academy

   | Records lost or damaged | Paper records can be misplaced, damaged by fire or water, or deteriorate over time. | Training history cannot be proven; retention rules are breached; operations are affected.
   | No version control | Multiple versions of the same document exist in different places. | Outdated data is used; confusion during assessment checks.
   | Hard to search and retrieve | Finding an old record means manually reviewing several sources and folders. | Delays in reporting, verification and inspection support.
   | Compliance & audit risk | No recorded edit or approval history; records are scattered across departments and formats. | Record validity cannot be proven during audit; dossiers take a long time to compile.
   | Inconsistent data | The same information is re-entered on several forms and files. | Data discrepancies and inaccurate statistics.
   | Low data security | Paper records can be viewed or copied without authorisation. | Personal data leakage and breaches of data-security rules.
[Google Shape;62;p7]: Why it matters: these are not only inefficiencies — in a regulated aviation environment they become compliance and safety risks. The ETR system was built to remove them.

[Google Shape;69;p8]: Addressing aviation training pain points through a centralized, tamper-evident digital platform
[Google Shape;71;p8]: Electronic Training Record (ETR)  •  SU26SE104_GSU48

[Google Shape;121;p12]: In scope — delivered
Aviation Training Lifecycle: 100% digitized workflow: Syllabus → Scheduling → Attendance (80% gate)→ Grading → Sign-off → QA → Manager Approval & Certification.

Data Integrity & Security: Automated SHA-256 evidence hashing, multi-tier record locking, JWT authentication & BCrypt security.

Aviation Audit Trail: Append-only logging of 100% sensitive data mutations with GMT+7 timestamps for CAAV inspection.

Batch Excel Processing: Validated template download & batch import for accounts, courses, classes, and student rosters.

Dedicated Web Portals: 7 specialized, responsive web portals for all actors (optimized for Desktop & Tablet).
[Google Shape;122;p12]: Out of scope (stated exclusions)
Financial & Tuition Billing: Financial billing is managed by external enterprise ERP/Accounting systems; decoupled from academic records.

Real-time Flight Telemetry: ETR records certified post-flight debriefings; live blackbox sensor streams belong to onboard avionics.

Native Mobile App Stores: Prioritized secure, zero-install responsive Web/PWA over app store distribution overhead.
[Google Shape;123;p12]: Strategic Focus: By strictly scoping out billing and flight telemetry, the system achieves enterprise-grade depth in training verification, regulatory compliance, and tamper-proof auditing.

[Google Shape;278;p23]: Limitations & Future Work
[Google Shape;279;p23]: We state the limitations before the panel has to point them out
[Google Shape;281;p23]: Electronic Training Record (ETR)  •  SU26SE104_GSU48
[Google Shape;282;p23]: Limitations (current scope)
Web-based only — a stable network connection and service availability are required.
Legacy paper / Excel records are not migrated automatically; migration must be a controlled import.
Learner access is read-only on official records — learners cannot create, verify or approve them.
Data accuracy depends on correct data entry by staff and on evidence verification.
No LMS / e-learning delivery is provided by the system.
No integration with external aviation authorities, airlines or third-party platforms.
No native mobile application; no multi-factor authentication yet.
[Google Shape;283;p23]: Future development
Mobile application (Android / iOS) for instructors and learners.
LMS and online-examination integration.
Direct integration with CAA / airline training systems.
Advanced analytics and AI-based recommendations (e.g. at-risk learner prediction).
Automated legacy-data migration tooling.
Multi-factor authentication and enterprise SSO.
Offline evidence capture for flight-line and simulator environments.
